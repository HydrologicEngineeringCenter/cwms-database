What is a Rating?
=================

A **rating** is a mathematical relationship between one or several independent parameter(s)
and a dependent parameter. Depending on the location where a rating is used, the independent
parameter(s) used for a rating may be a height such as stage or elevation and additional
parameters such as the measurement of a gate's opening. The dependent parameter's values
result from the values defined for its related independent parameter(s). Whichever set of
independent and dependent parameters are used, a rating is a valuable tool in the processing
of surface water data for water management purposes.

For hydrologic systems, ratings are available for different locations and measured at
different times. Different locations experience changes to their physical characteristics,
especially for streams. For accuracy, stream gages' measurements are checked every 6 - 8 weeks
or more often when and where necessary. For reservoirs however, the rating for its elevation
and area may be updated every 10 years. There are also different types of ratings including
USGS Stream Ratings, which are discussed in :ref:`USGS Ratings <../rating-types/usgs-rating-type>`.
